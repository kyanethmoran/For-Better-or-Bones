For Better or Bones — Progress Notes

I started development on For Better or Bones, a cozy dark-fantasy dungeon crawler about a skeleton trying to restore his wife by finding her missing bones in dungeons. The game combines dungeon crawling, puzzles, light combat, resource gathering, and home restoration.

The main gameplay loop I am building toward is:
	Start at the house → enter a dungeon → collect bones/resources → return home → restore the house

So far, I have chosen Godot as the game engine, started the project, and built the first basic player prototype. I created a player scene with a placeholder body, collision, a camera attached to the player, and working movement controls. The player can now move in all directions and jump.

I also started setting up GitHub so I can track progress, keep a history of changes, and document the development process over time. I have also started planning how I will handle assets, using free CC0 packs for now and possibly creating custom low-poly assets later in Blender or similar tools.
